Nausicaa and her maids

John Flaxman · 1810 · Wikimedia Commons

Find the small ball up at the left and the helmeted Athena hovering unseen behind the players: the goddess steers the game so the ball flies wide into the river and the girls' shout wakes Odysseus (Od. 6.110-117). Flaxman lines the maids up mid-throw, mantles
