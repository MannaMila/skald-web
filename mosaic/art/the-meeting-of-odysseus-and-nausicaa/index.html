The Meeting of Odysseus and Nausicaa

Jacob Jordaens · c. 1630–1640 · Rijksmuseum · Amsterdam, Netherlands

Washed ashore and exhausted, Odysseus surprises Princess Nausicaa and her maids doing laundry; she kindly gives him food and fresh clothes. Explore its place in Book VI of the Odyssey.
