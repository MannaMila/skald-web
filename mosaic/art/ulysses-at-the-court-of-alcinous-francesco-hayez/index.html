Ulysses at the Court of Alcinous

Francesco Hayez · ca. 1813 · Collezione d'arte della Banca d'Italia

Alcinous sits in red at the center as the Phaeacian court leans toward Odysseus; light from the left gathers the figures around the table. Explore its place in Book VII of the Odyssey.
