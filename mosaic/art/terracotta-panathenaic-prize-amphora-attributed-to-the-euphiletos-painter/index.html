Terracotta Panathenaic prize amphora

Attributed to the Euphiletos Painter · ca. 530 BCE · The Metropolitan Museum of Art

At Patroclus’s games, Ajax leads the footrace with Odysseus close behind him (23.759–760). Runners are painted on this prize jar. Explore its place in Homer's Iliad, Book XXIII, line 759.
