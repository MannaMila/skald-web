Antigone Pouring a Libation over the Corpse of Her Brother Polynices

William Henry Rinehart · 1867–70; carved 1870 · met

A marble Antigone tips a jug held overhead, pouring a libation over her brother Polynices, as the guard reports that someone has buried the corpse and performed the rites piety demands (245). Explore its place in Sophocles's Plays, Antigone, line 238.
