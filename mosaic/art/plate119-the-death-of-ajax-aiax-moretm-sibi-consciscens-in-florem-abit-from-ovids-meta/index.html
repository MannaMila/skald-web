Plate119: The Death of Ajax (Aiax moretm Sibi consciscens, in florem abit), from Ovid's 'Metamorphoses'

Antonio Tempesta · 1606 · met

Ajax falls on his sword, his shield on the ground, in an etching for Ovid's Metamorphoses. Here Ajax speaks over the sword, Hector's gift, fixed in Trojan soil (815). Explore its place in Sophocles's Plays, Ajax, line 785.
