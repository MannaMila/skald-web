Plate 143: Polyphemus Eating Ulysses' Men, as Achaemides Watches (Ulyssis socios deuorat Polyphemus), from Ovid's 'Metamorphoses'

Antonio Tempesta · 1606 · met

Tempesta's etching for Ovid shows the one-eyed giant eating a man among the bodies of his victims, the meal Odysseus describes to the satyrs. (400) Explore its place in Euripides's Plays, Cyclops, line 417.
