Terracotta neck-amphora (jar) of Panathenaic shape

Attributed to the Antimenes Painter · ca. 520 BCE · met

A terracotta jar shows two boxers facing each other as onlookers watch, echoing Socrates' comparison of Euthydemus and Dionysodorus to all-round fighters (271c) Explore its place in Plato's Dialogues, Euthydemus, section 3.
