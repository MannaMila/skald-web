Odysseus and Nausicaa

Jacob Jordaens · 1630–1635 · Het Noordbrabants Museum · 's-Hertogenbosch, Netherlands

In Jordaens’s first small version of the scene, shipwrecked Odysseus steps forward while brave Nausicaa keeps her frightened maids calm. Explore its place in Book VI of the Odyssey.
