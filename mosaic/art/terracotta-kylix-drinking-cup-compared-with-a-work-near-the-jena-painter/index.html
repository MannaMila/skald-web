Terracotta kylix (drinking cup)

Compared with a work near the Jena Painter · ca. 350 BCE · met

A winged figure drives a horse-drawn chariot on a terracotta drinking cup, offering a visual parallel to Socrates' figure of the soul as a charioteer with winged horses. (246a) Explore its place in Plato's Dialogues, Phaedrus, section 95.
