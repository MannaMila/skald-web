Terracotta kylix (drinking cup)

Attributed to a painter of the Thorvaldsen Group · ca. 500 BCE · met

A drinking cup shows a figure lifting a spear near a helmet and shield, with a boy beside him, as Lysimachus asks whether his sons should learn armed fighting (181c) Explore its place in Plato's Dialogues, Laches, section 15.
