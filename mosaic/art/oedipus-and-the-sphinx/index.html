Oedipus and the Sphinx

Gustave Moreau · 1864 · met

Moreau paints Oedipus meeting the winged Sphinx on a rocky pass, the riddling 'watchful dog' whose song Oedipus alone could answer when the seer stayed silent (392). Explore its place in Sophocles's Plays, Oedipus Tyrannus, line 379.
