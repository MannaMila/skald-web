Plate 124: Hecuba and the Trojan Women Murdering Polymestor (Hecuba Polymnestori oculos ervit), from Ovid's 'Metamorphoses'

Antonio Tempesta (Italian, Florence 1555–1630 Rome) · 1606 · met

Trojan women pin Polymestor to the ground and tear at his eyes while one swings a club. An illustration to Ovid, it matches Polymestor's own account of the blinding (1170–1174). Explore its place in Euripides's Plays, Hecuba, line 1151.
