Bracelet with goat's head terminals

Achaemenid · ca. 6th–4th century BCE · met

A gold Achaemenid bracelet ending in two goat heads. After Plataea the helots stripped the Persian dead of their armlets, torques and golden daggers (9.80). Explore its place in Herodotus's Histories, Book IX, chapter 215.
