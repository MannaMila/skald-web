Terracotta oinochoe (jug)

Attributed to the Disney Painter · ca. 430–420 BCE · The Metropolitan Museum of Art · New York, USA

The Met identifies this weathered archer as probably Odysseus. His unkempt hair, together with parallels in fuller ancient scenes, is the clue to the wanderer behind the bow. Explore its place in Book XXII of the Odyssey.
