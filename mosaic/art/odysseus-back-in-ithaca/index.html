Odysseus back in Ithaca

John Flaxman · 1810 · Wikimedia Commons

The plate labels itself 'Odyssee Liv. XIII': two Phaeacian sailors lower the sleeping Odysseus in his cloak while a third holds a folded robe, and his gift-treasure - a tripod-cauldron, jars, a chest - is stacked at right, just as Homer lists it set beside him
