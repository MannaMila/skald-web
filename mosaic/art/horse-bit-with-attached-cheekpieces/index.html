Horse bit with attached cheekpieces

Achaemenid · ca. 6th–4th century BCE · met

A bronze Achaemenid horse bit with its cheekpieces. Masistius, who led Mardonius' cavalry against the Greeks, rode a Nesaean horse with a golden bit and rich trappings (9.20). Explore its place in Herodotus's Histories, Book IX, chapter 52.
