Achilles waarschuwt Patroclus

Crispijn van de Passe (I) · 1613 · rijksmuseum

Achilles gestures toward an armoured Patroclus beside the Greek tents as battle unfolds behind them; he tells Patroclus to return once he has driven the Trojans from the ships (16.87). Explore its place in Homer's Iliad, Book XVI, line 87.
