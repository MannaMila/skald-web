De redding van Oedipus

Salvator Rosa (mentioned on object) · 1625 - 1673 · rijksmuseum

An etching of the rescue of Oedipus: the infant hangs from a tree, bound by his feet, as a man unties him, the deed the Corinthian messenger now recalls (1034). Explore its place in Sophocles's Plays, Oedipus Tyrannus, line 995.
