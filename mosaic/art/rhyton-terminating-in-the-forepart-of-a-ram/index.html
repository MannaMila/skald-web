Rhyton terminating in the forepart of a ram

Achaemenid · ca. 5th century BCE · met

A silver Achaemenid rhyton ending in the forepart of a ram. Amestris waits for Xerxes' annual royal banquet, held on the day he became king (9.110). Explore its place in Herodotus's Histories, Book IX, chapter 290.
