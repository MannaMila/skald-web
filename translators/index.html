Twenty-four translations, one poem

The Translators.

Behind each Odyssey in Skald is a translator — or, in two cases, a collaboration. Open a card to meet them, see how they came to Homer, and learn what their version brings to the poem. Nineteen of the twenty-four cards show portraits with a recorded public-domain basis; five use plaques: three because no locatable public-domain portrait was found, and two because U.S. public-domain status remains unconfirmed.
