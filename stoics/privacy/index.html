Skald: The Stoics — Privacy Policy

Status: published at https://skald.mannamila.com/stoics/privacy/ on 2026-10-30 (round-2 reviewed text)

Effective date: The date Skald: The Stoics is first made available

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Canonical URL: https://skald.mannamila.com/stoics/privacy/

This policy applies to every version of Skald: The Stoics. Your version is shown at the bottom of About & Privacy. Skald: Odyssey, MannaMila's other reader, has its own policy.

Who we are

Skald: The Stoics is a reader for Marcus Aurelius, Epictetus and Seneca, with Diogenes Laertius' Lives of the Stoics and Cleanthes' Hymn to Zeus, published by MannaMila LLC. Contact privacy@mannamila.com about privacy or data requests, or support@mannamila.com for product support.

At a glance

  • Reading text, your saved place, selected book and translation, and reading preferences are kept in app storage. MannaMila has no Skald account or reading-history server. Platform backup and device transfer are described below.
  • The app configures no advertising measurement or analytics event reporting. It never asks for Apple's tracking permission, creates no Analytics ID for you, and displays no ads.
  • When the app classifies your device as outside the European Union, it can check MannaMila's content service for reviewed library updates no more than once an hour. That request carries no reading data. When it classifies your device as in the European Union, it makes no such request and reads the library already on the device. This classification uses device settings and, on Android, mobile-network and SIM signs rather than physical location. If you are in the EU but none identifies an EU country, the app may make an update request; see “If your device shows none of these signs” below.
  • Reading reminders are scheduled locally. You control them through your device's notification settings.
  • Apple or Google handles purchases. The app receives ownership information, not your payment-card or bank details.

Reading data and reminders

Your reading progress, preferences and note-interaction history are stored in the app's private storage. They let the app return to your place and adapt which notes it shows. MannaMila has no server that receives or synchronizes this history. The app includes no analytics or crash-reporting service.

The app can send up to twelve reminders in all: three days after you last used it, a week after, and then every two weeks. Each use starts the sequence again; opening the app before the three-day reminder prevents that reminder. A reminder is titled "Continue reading the Stoics…" and usually quotes the passage you will return to. If it cannot find that passage, it uses the fixed opening sentence of Meditations I.1 in George Long's translation. The reminder is scheduled on your device without a push service; its excerpt is not sent to MannaMila. Your device may show the excerpt in a notification preview, including on a lock screen if previews are enabled. You can hide previews or turn reminders off in your device's notification settings. Tapping a reminder opens the reader at your saved place; the quoted fallback sentence may differ from that place.

The app asks for permission to send notifications at most once, and only after you have finished a chapter by scrolling through it or have spent five minutes in the app. On Android the system asks only on Android 13 and later. You can turn reminders off at any time in your device's notification settings.

On Android, Google backup or device transfer may preserve the file that records your full access, depending on your device and backup settings. Reading progress, preferences, downloaded content and all other app data are excluded. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the active local copy; a platform restore may restore the full-access record.

On iPhone and iPad, Apple backup and device transfer may preserve reading progress, preferences, the local full-access record and a local checkout record according to your Apple settings; downloaded library updates are excluded from backup. Uninstalling removes the active local copy; a later Apple restore may restore that data. Purchase ownership can also be recovered from the same store and store account.

No advertising measurement or tracking

Skald: The Stoics does not measure advertising, and neither its Android nor its iPhone and iPad version contains a Meta SDK. MannaMila does not configure the app to send advertising or analytics events, and the app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. On Android, Google Play Billing may communicate with Google as part of a purchase, as described below.

On iPhone and iPad, the app never asks for Apple's tracking permission, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and submits no SKAdNetwork conversion values.

On Android, the app does not read Google's advertising ID or the Play install referrer, and it declares no advertising permissions.

Purchases

Some works and sections are free. A one-time purchase opens the rest of the library at a standard price or a student or teacher price. The latter is chosen by self-attestation; the app does not ask for documents or proof of enrolment or employment.

Apple or Google processes payment and returns purchase or ownership information to the app. The app saves a local record of your full access before it acknowledges or finishes the transaction. It has no purchase server and does not receive your card, bank account or payment method. When you start a checkout, the app keeps the product, the price the store displayed, its currency and the time on the device to match the store's answer. The app does not send that checkout record to MannaMila or an advertising service; Apple backup or device transfer may preserve it on iPhone and iPad, as described above.

Separately from the app, the stores report sales to MannaMila in every country. Apple reports sales totals. Google Play provides order-level accounting reports with order number, product, time, price, tax and some device and area details. See “What MannaMila can still receive” below for the full list and how long MannaMila keeps its copies.

Restore purchase uses the same store and store account. Apple and Google process their store services under their own privacy notices.

Content updates

At most once an hour when you open the app and it classifies your device as outside the European Union, it can contact https://delivery.mannamila.com, a MannaMila service hosted on Cloudflare, for reviewed library updates, app settings and in-app notes. There is no separate in-app switch. Requests and downloads use HTTPS. A failed request leaves the existing library available.

The app treats a device as in the European Union when its region setting or time zone says so, or on Android when its current mobile network's country or its SIM's country does. It reads those signs on the device when it first needs a region decision in the current app process, keeps that decision until the process restarts, and does not store or send the signs. Such a device does not contact the content service at all and reads the library that shipped with the app or was installed earlier.

The initial update check names only the app and its update channel. If an update is available, the app requests download tokens for pack paths in the verified manifest and downloads those packs. These requests carry no reading history, saved place, passage, selected book or translation, advertising ID or purchase record. Routine network information such as the request IP address is processed to serve and protect the request. The service uses the address as a rate-limit key. MannaMila does not retain the address. Your device's networking software may add details such as its operating-system version and, on Android, its device model. Downloaded content is checked before it can replace the active library.

Separate website, links and messages

If you contact us by email, we receive your message, address and any information you choose to include. We offer no Stoics reader feedback form or updates mailing list at launch. Our public website is separate from the app and may process routine web-request information and website analytics. Opening a link in the app — to a source edition, a licence or a scholarly reference — uses that site's services and privacy terms. Sharing a passage or picture hands it to the app you choose in your device's share sheet.

Your choices and security

Reset reading progress starts your reading again without removing what you have unlocked. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data, subject to platform backup and device-transfer settings described above. You can turn local reading reminders off in your device's notification settings.

Network requests described here use encrypted HTTPS. The app does not ask for your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or general file storage. Android permits no cleartext network traffic. No security measure is absolute; contact privacy@mannamila.com about a suspected privacy or security problem.

Skald: The Stoics is meant for teenage and adult readers and is not directed at children under 13. Its App Store age rating and Google Play regional content ratings will appear on their respective listings. The app is not intended for Google Play's Designed for Families program.

MannaMila plans to offer Skald: The Stoics on Google Play and the App Store in the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and the 27 member states of the European Union. Actual availability depends on the store listings.

If you are in the European Union

On a device that the app treats as in the European Union, it contacts no MannaMila server. It never sends anything to Meta, in the European Union or anywhere else. The app treats a device as in the European Union when one of these signs says so: its region setting or its time zone, or on Android its current mobile network's country or its SIM's country.

MannaMila LLC, a company in the United States, is responsible for the personal data it receives or decides the use of. European Union law calls that the “controller”. MannaMila has no office in the European Union and has not appointed a representative there at this time. Write to privacy@mannamila.com with any privacy question or request.

What MannaMila can still receive

  • Messages you send us: your email address and what you write. We use them to answer you and to keep a record of what was asked and done. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering the people who write to us; when you use a right listed below, the law requires us to act. We keep a message until your question or request is settled and for as long afterwards as we need a record to respond to follow-up, document our response, resolve a dispute or meet a legal duty. We delete it when none of those reasons applies. Google hosts our mailbox.
  • Sales records, if you buy the unlock. As noted under “Purchases”, Apple or Google sells you the unlock and reports sales to MannaMila in every country. Apple reports totals. Google Play reports each order: its number, time, product, price and tax, device model and country, sometimes the state, city and postal code. Its reports do not have your name, email address or payment details, but we can find an order by its number. We keep our copies while United States tax rules require accounting records and delete them when those obligations expire, unless a dispute or other legal duty requires continued retention. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in keeping proper accounts.
  • Store reviews. A review you post on the App Store or Google Play is public. We may read and answer it to support readers; our legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to feedback. Google Play also shows us the device model, Android version and language it came from. Apple and Google control how long public reviews remain visible. MannaMila does not keep separate copies of store reviews.
  • The website. It is separate from the app; see “Separate website, links and messages”.

What you can ask for, and how

You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to correct or delete it, or to restrict it (we keep it but stop using it). Where we use it with your consent, you can ask for it in a form you can take elsewhere.

Where the legal basis is our legitimate interest, you can object at any time.

Email privacy@mannamila.com and describe your request. If you contacted us before, using the same address helps us find your message. For a Google Play order, include its receipt order number. We may ask for enough information to identify the record. We will explain if we hold no identifiable record or must keep one for a legal obligation. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to the data-protection authority where you live or work, or where you think the law was broken. The European Data Protection Board lists the authorities at https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en.

If your device shows none of these signs

This part is only for a device that is in the European Union although none of the signs above says so. A time zone set to a place in the European Union rules it out. The app treats such a device as outside the European Union and can check the content service for library updates, as “Content updates” describes. The service then processes the request's IP address to serve and protect the request, as “Content updates” describes. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in an up-to-date library.

To leave this case, set your device's time zone or region to where you are, then fully quit the app so its process ends, and relaunch it.
