Statistical comparison

Against its siblings

Wilster’s complete Danish Odyssey combines hexameter with archaism, colloquial ease, and Romantic coloring, and the atlas records a long history of reprinting. The corpus contains 122876 tokens and 10095 unique forms. Its score-ranked formula list begins with “saa han taled og,” recorded 58 times; this first position does not mean it has the highest raw count. For scale within other hexameter verse translations, Voß contains 109003 tokens. This is a bounded comparison: the history identifies the translation’s formal setting, while the statistics locate its scale and verbal patterning among the peers actually declared in the source. These measures offer a compact view of the textual evidence available here. The emphasis remains on directly observable features of the supplied corpora. That focus keeps the comparison useful without asking the data to support more than it can.
