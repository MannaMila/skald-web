Statistical comparison

Against its siblings

Hobbes approached Homer late in life, after his political works, producing a complete rhymed Odyssey that the atlas characterizes through its direct narrative emphasis. The corpus contains 93977 tokens and 5915 unique forms. Its score-ranked formula list begins with “and to the,” recorded 35 times; this first position does not mean it has the highest raw count. For scale within other English translations, Murray contains 133986 tokens. Read together, the atlas context and corpus evidence show how this version occupies its declared cohort without converting length, vocabulary counts, or recurrence into claims about merit, fidelity, intention, or reception. These measures offer a compact view of the textual evidence available here. The emphasis remains on directly observable features of the supplied corpora.
