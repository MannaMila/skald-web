Statistical comparison

Against its siblings

Cotterill offers a sustained English experiment in hexameter, using long rolling lines to engage directly with the meter associated with Homer in the atlas account. The corpus contains 145162 tokens and 8231 unique forms. Its score-ranked formula list begins with “all of the,” recorded 169 times; this first position does not mean it has the highest raw count. For scale within other English translations, Murray contains 133986 tokens. The comparison therefore joins publication history to observable corpus structure, keeping the historical portrait vivid while letting the measurements describe only textual scale, lexical distribution, and repeated phrasing. These measures offer a compact view of the textual evidence available here. The emphasis remains on directly observable features of the supplied corpora. That focus keeps the comparison useful without asking the data to support more than it can.
