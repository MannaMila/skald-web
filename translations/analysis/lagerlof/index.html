Statistical comparison

Against its siblings

Lagerlöf’s Swedish hexameters occupy a durable place in the atlas, later modernized and also reissued in their original form by the Swedish Academy. The corpus contains 122053 tokens and 11031 unique forms. Its score-ranked formula list begins with “svarade då den,” recorded 118 times; this first position does not mean it has the highest raw count. For scale within other hexameter verse translations, Voß contains 109003 tokens. The result is a clearer profile of form and corpus patterning, grounded in the stated comparison group and careful not to treat measured differences as judgments about literary success or accuracy. These measures offer a compact view of the textual evidence available here. The emphasis remains on directly observable features of the supplied corpora.
