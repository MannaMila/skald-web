Statistical comparison

Against its siblings

Palmer’s American prose Odyssey grew from a partial publication into a complete classroom text, framed by the atlas as supple and faintly rhythmic in construction. The corpus contains 116728 tokens and 6545 unique forms. Its score-ranked formula list begins with “the son of,” recorded 106 times; this first position does not mean it has the highest raw count. For scale within other English translations, Murray contains 133986 tokens. The result is a clearer profile of form and corpus patterning, grounded in the stated comparison group and careful not to treat measured differences as judgments about literary success or accuracy. These measures offer a compact view of the textual evidence available here. The emphasis remains on directly observable features of the supplied corpora.
