Statistical comparison

Against its siblings

Johnston’s blank-verse Odyssey moved from an online edition to print and audio before entering the public domain, a publication path unusually visible in the atlas. The corpus contains 129664 tokens and 6755 unique forms. Its score-ranked formula list begins with “answered him and said,” recorded 80 times; this first position does not mean it has the highest raw count. For scale within other English translations, Murray contains 133986 tokens. Read together, the atlas context and corpus evidence show how this version occupies its declared cohort without converting length, vocabulary counts, or recurrence into claims about merit, fidelity, intention, or reception. These measures offer a compact view of the textual evidence available here. The emphasis remains on directly observable features of the supplied corpora.
