Statistical comparison

Against its siblings

Vosmaer’s Dutch hexameter Odyssey, completed near the end of his life, joined his Iliad as a paired Homer that remained prominent for decades. The corpus contains 128485 tokens and 11715 unique forms. Its score-ranked formula list begins with “alzoo sprak hij,” recorded 126 times; this first position does not mean it has the highest raw count. For scale within other hexameter verse translations, Voß contains 109003 tokens. Read together, the atlas context and corpus evidence show how this version occupies its declared cohort without converting length, vocabulary counts, or recurrence into claims about merit, fidelity, intention, or reception.
