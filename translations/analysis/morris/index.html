Statistical comparison

Against its siblings

Morris turns the Odyssey toward northern romance in the atlas, using long rhymed couplets and a deliberately saga-colored vocabulary that includes “menfolk” and “fellow-farers.” The corpus contains 147844 tokens and 6390 unique forms. Its score-ranked formula list begins with “he spake and,” recorded 122 times; this first position does not mean it has the highest raw count. For scale within other English translations, Murray contains 133986 tokens. Read together, the atlas context and corpus evidence show how this version occupies its declared cohort without converting length, vocabulary counts, or recurrence into claims about merit, fidelity, intention, or reception. These measures offer a compact view of the textual evidence available here. The emphasis remains on directly observable features of the supplied corpora.
