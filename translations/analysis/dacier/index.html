Statistical comparison

Against its siblings

Dacier’s learned French prose emerged amid the dispute between Ancients and Moderns, and her scholarship later informed Pope even where their judgments diverged. The corpus contains 143271 tokens and 9962 unique forms. Its score-ranked formula list begins with “en même temps,” recorded 91 times; this first position does not mean it has the highest raw count. For scale within other Français translations, Leconte de Lisle contains 118797 tokens. The result is a clearer profile of form and corpus patterning, grounded in the stated comparison group and careful not to treat measured differences as judgments about literary success or accuracy. These measures offer a compact view of the textual evidence available here. The emphasis remains on directly observable features of the supplied corpora.
