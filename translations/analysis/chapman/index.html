Statistical comparison

Against its siblings

Chapman’s Odyssey completes the first full English Homer in the atlas, recasting the poem in expansive rhymed pentameter and a vocabulary of wisdom and pilgrimage. The corpus contains 140625 tokens and 8434 unique forms. Its score-ranked formula list begins with “of all the,” recorded 67 times; this first position does not mean it has the highest raw count. For scale within other English translations, Murray contains 133986 tokens. Read together, the atlas context and corpus evidence show how this version occupies its declared cohort without converting length, vocabulary counts, or recurrence into claims about merit, fidelity, intention, or reception. These measures offer a compact view of the textual evidence available here. The emphasis remains on directly observable features of the supplied corpora.
