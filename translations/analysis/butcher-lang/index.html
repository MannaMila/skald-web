Statistical comparison

Against its siblings

Butcher and Lang represent the Victorian prose standard in the atlas, with deliberately archaic, scriptural coloring that later translators used as a point of contrast. The corpus contains 135560 tokens and 6093 unique forms. Its score-ranked formula list begins with “odysseus of many counsels,” recorded 66 times; this first position does not mean it has the highest raw count. For scale within other English translations, Murray contains 133986 tokens. This is a bounded comparison: the history identifies the translation’s formal setting, while the statistics locate its scale and verbal patterning among the peers actually declared in the source. These measures offer a compact view of the textual evidence available here. The emphasis remains on directly observable features of the supplied corpora.
