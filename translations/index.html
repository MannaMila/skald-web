The Odyssey, in every voice

One poem, twenty-eight centuries of retellings. This atlas charts the Odyssey's translations—from Livius Andronicus's Latin Odusia to the newest English verse—with their lineages, famous lines, U.S. rights status, and, where a copy has been digitized, where to read it free. Translations bundled in the Skald reader are marked In Skald.
