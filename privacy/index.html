Skald: Odyssey — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-28
Last updated: 2026-07-28
Canonical URL: https://skald.mannamila.com/privacy/

Who we are

Skald: Odyssey is a reader for Homer's Odyssey, with source-grounded retellings, historical editions, Greek text, maps, and art. The app is published by MannaMila LLC ("MannaMila", "we", "us", or "our").

For privacy or data requests, contact privacy@mannamila.com. For product support, contact support@mannamila.com.

App data

Skald stores your reading position and preferences on your device. The app has no account and no MannaMila server that syncs this reading data.

Beginning with version 0.4.1, Skald does not send usage analytics or crash diagnostics. It does not initialize Amplitude or Sentry, create an Analytics ID, or operate a telemetry upload queue. MannaMila does not receive reading activity or crash diagnostics from version 0.4.1.

Previously shared data

Earlier versions of Skald could send pseudonymous usage analytics to Amplitude and crash or diagnostic records to Sentry. An installation upgraded from an earlier version may retain the random Analytics ID created at that time.

Version 0.4.1 reads that existing ID only to display it in About & Privacy and, at your request, place it in a deletion-request email. The app does not transmit the ID or create a replacement. If no earlier ID exists, Skald displays “No Analytics ID exists on this installation.”

To request deletion of earlier Amplitude and Sentry records, use “Request deletion of previously shared data” in About & Privacy or email privacy@mannamila.com with the Analytics ID. Existing records remain subject to the retention periods stated below until they are deleted or expire.

Separate services

This policy’s no-telemetry statement concerns data sent by the Skald app. It does not mean that no data is processed when you use a separate service.

If you email support or privacy, submit the public feedback form, join the product-updates list, or obtain Skald through Apple’s App Store or Google Play, those services may process information you choose to provide and routine technical or transaction information. The feedback and product-updates notices explain the corresponding MannaMila forms. Apple and Google handle their store services under their own privacy notices. None of those separate activities causes Skald 0.4.1 to send reading analytics or crash diagnostics.

The public Skald website is also separate from the app. Its hosting provider may process routine request information needed to deliver the site. The version prepared for this launch does not deploy MannaMila's shared Google Analytics tag. The release record must contain live-site evidence before any country is cleared.

Local reading data and platform backup

Skald stores your exact saved place and reading settings, such as the selected length, translation, and display options, in the app's private on-device storage. The app has no account, reading-progress backend, or app-operated cloud sync.

Android excludes app data from cloud backup and device transfer. On iPhone and iPad, Apple's encrypted device or iCloud backup and device-transfer features may preserve app-container data according to your Apple settings. Clearing Android app data or uninstalling Skald removes the active local copy. A platform backup may preserve or restore a copy according to the platform's controls.

Data the version 0.4.1 app does not send

Version 0.4.1 does not send MannaMila:

  • your reading position, selected book, tier, translation, or reading activity;
  • product-interaction analytics, an Analytics ID, crash reports, stack traces, or device diagnostics;
  • your name, contacts, precise location, photos, audio, files, advertising ID, or activity in other apps.

Skald contains no advertising, ad network, cross-app tracking, or cross-site tracking. It does not use Android's advertising ID or Apple's Identifier for Advertisers and does not request an App Tracking Transparency permission.

Information you choose to send separately

If you contact support or privacy, we receive the message, contact address, and other information you choose to include. Do not include a story passage, screenshot, log, or other personal information unless it is necessary for the request.

The feedback form and product-updates list have separate notices at the point of collection. Apple and Google independently process store-account, transaction, and routine technical information when they provide their store services. Their privacy notices govern that independent processing.

Historical telemetry retention

The prior release record documented an Amplitude retention period of up to 24 months and a Sentry retention period of 90 days for records sent by earlier versions. Those historical periods and the current deletion state of the provider accounts are pending owner recheck before UK launch. They must not be treated as confirmed current provider settings until the versioned UK compliance record contains dated evidence.

On-device reading data persists until you clear app storage or uninstall the app, subject to the platform-backup behavior described above. Retention for support messages, forms, website delivery, and store records is also pending the documented UK processing-inventory review.

Security

Version 0.4.1 has no app telemetry transport. Release artifacts are scanned for Amplitude and Sentry runtime markers, telemetry credentials, upload endpoints, telemetry queues, and telemetry-named payloads before distribution.

On-device reading progress and preferences remain in the app's private storage. No security measure is absolute. If you think information you sent through a separate service has been affected by a security incident, contact privacy@mannamila.com.

Children

Skald is intended for a general/adult audience (18+). A parent or caregiver may use it for shared reading, but the adult is the intended operator. It is not directed to children under 13 and is not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families program.

The version 0.4.1 app has no account and sends no usage analytics or crash diagnostics. Separate support, form, website, and store services remain subject to their stated notices.
